🌱 Welcome to KARAKUNKU FARM SOLAR-AGRICULTURAL ENTERPRISE

Karakunku Farm is a transformative solar‑powered agricultural demonstration and service enterprise based in Jarra Barrow Kunda, Lower River Region, The Gambia. We address two critical challenges: energy poverty in farming communities and the severe seasonal shortage of fresh vegetables caused by reliance on rain‑fed agriculture.

Our dual business model operates a commercial vegetable farm using solar‑powered irrigation and cold storage for year‑round production, while also serving as a solar service provider – demonstrating, selling, installing, and maintaining solar water pumps, home systems, and cold storage for other farmers and rural households.

Our farm is a living laboratory where farmers see solar technology working in real conditions. This “seeing‑is‑believing” approach is our most powerful marketing tool and addresses the primary barrier to solar adoption in rural areas.

📖 About Us

KARAKUNKU FARM SOLAR-AGRICULTURAL ENTERPRISE was established in 2020 by Lamin Ceesay, a practical farmer with over five years of hands‑on experience in vegetable production and farm management. The farm is situated on fertile land in Jarra Barrow Kunda, an area with strong agricultural potential but severely limited access to water infrastructure and electricity.

Through multiple planting cycles, we have gained invaluable experience in crop selection, market timing, production planning, and customer relationship management. We have built a loyal customer base among local households, market vendors, and food businesses.

Vision: To become The Gambia’s leading provider of solar‑powered agricultural solutions, transforming rural farming communities through clean, affordable, and reliable energy access.

Mission: To empower rural Gambian farmers with solar technology that enables sustainable, profitable agriculture while operating a commercially viable enterprise that demonstrates the power of clean energy.

Legal Structure: Sole proprietorship formally registered in The Gambia under the ownership of Lamin Ceesay, with a dedicated bank account and proper financial records.

⚠️ The Challenge We Solve

Rural Gambia faces three interconnected challenges that Karakunku Farm addresses:

  • Energy Problem: Over 60% of rural households lack electricity. Farmers suffer high costs from diesel dependency – a typical pump consumes 500 litres per season, costing 25,000–40,000 GMD annually.
  • Food Problem: The Gambia imports over 60% of its food. Most farmers rely on rainfall, leading to severe seasonal patterns – vegetable oversupply in the rainy season and acute shortages in the dry season, causing price volatility and food insecurity.
  • Knowledge Gap: Rural farmers lack access to affordable, reliable solar products and trustworthy installation services. Many have been burned by low‑quality products or poor installation.

Our integrated approach solves these problems by demonstrating, distributing, installing, and maintaining solar technology while producing fresh vegetables year‑round.

💡 Our Solution

  • Demonstration: Our farm is a living showroom where farmers see solar irrigation, lighting, and cold storage working in real agricultural conditions.
  • Distribution: We source high‑quality, affordable solar equipment and offer flexible payment options through microfinance partnerships.
  • Installation and Maintenance: Our trained technicians provide professional installation and ongoing maintenance services.
  • Agricultural Production: Our farmers produce and supply fresh vegetables year‑round using solar‑powered irrigation, demonstrating commercial viability and meeting local demand.

This integrated approach builds trust, reduces barriers to adoption, and creates a sustainable cycle of clean energy and food production.

📊 Market Opportunity

Target Customers:

  • Primary (Solar): Smallholder farmers, rural off‑grid households, women’s farming cooperatives, and community gardens in Lower River Region.
  • Secondary (Vegetables): Local households, market vendors in Soma, retail shops, food businesses (restaurants, caterers), and institutional buyers (schools, hospitals).

Competitive Advantage: Our unique combination of farming expertise and solar knowledge, rural presence, demonstration farm, after‑sales support, and local trust gives us an unbeatable edge over urban‑based solar companies and seasonal smallholder farmers.

Market Trends: Rising demand for fresh vegetables, growing preference for local produce, government support for renewable energy, increasing fuel costs, and climate change making rain‑fed farming unreliable all work in our favour.

⚙️ Operations & Production

Agricultural Products: We produce hot pepper, okra, onion, and cucumber using solar‑powered drip irrigation. Year‑round production ensures consistent supply.

Solar Products & Services: We offer solar water pumps, solar home systems, solar cold storage, and complete farm electrification packages. We also provide professional installation, maintenance, and training.

Production Process: Land preparation → planting → irrigation (solar drip) → crop management → harvesting → post‑harvest handling (cold storage) → distribution.

Solar Service Process: Customer identification → needs assessment → system design → sales & financing → installation → after‑sales support.

Our farm serves as a demonstration facility where visitors see all systems working together in an integrated, sustainable model.
